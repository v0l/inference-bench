## #815 notes

Change kept: `split_activations = false` in `family-qwen3_5.toml` (prefill 1404 to 2180 at 1024, ratio 0.54 to 0.89 against llama.cpp, see docs/perf/bf16-activation-envelope.md for the accuracy against fp32).

GEMM tiles for the bf16 big kernels (Spark, 1024 and 4096 tokens, 2 rounds): the 256x128 (4x2) tile that gate_up (n 24576) already uses gives 2020-2160 tok/s. Forcing the big tile via `big_tile` is slower everywhere: 128x256 (2x4) 1470-1545, 128x128 (2x4) 1440-1490, 128x256 (4x2) 1535-1555, 128x128 (4x2) 1500-1550; 256x128 as an override fails to load because the MTP fp4 big kernel does not fit shared memory. Lowering `BF16_BIG_MIN_N` from 8192 to 4096 (so lin_in, qkv and o use 256x128 too) paired A/B: 1.025 at 1024 and 0.993 at 4096, IQR 0.15 and 0.23, below the 5% bar, not kept.

The GEMM is already the #813 tile; the remaining gap is the mainloop (see docs/perf/llama/2026-11-19.md). The delta-net kernels are #884.
