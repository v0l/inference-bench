## qwen3_next

`nvidia/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct-NVFP4` (48 GB, modelopt NVFP4 group 16) fits the gate. specrt refused it at `config_groups.group_0`; the `family-base.toml` modelopt rule now accepts that group. It then fails loading: `model.layers.0.linear_attn.in_proj_qkv.weight is not in the checkpoint`, because Qwen3-Next stores the fused interleaved `in_proj_qkvz` and `in_proj_ba` where the qwen3_5 family expects split `in_proj_qkv`, `in_proj_z`. Not scored.
