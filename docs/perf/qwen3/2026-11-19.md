Gap check: nsys kernel sum 894.81 ms over 126 steps is 7.10 ms per step; wall step at 146 tok/s is 6.85 ms (graph replay overlaps launch gaps). There is no host gap to fix.

Per kernel bytes over median time (bf16 weights): lm_head 311 MB in 1343 us = 232 GB/s (85% of 273), gate_up 12.6 MB in 57 us = 221 GB/s (81%), qkv 8.4 MB in 38.6 us = 218 GB/s (80%), down 6.3 MB in 34.8 us = 181 GB/s (66%), o 4.2 MB in 23.3 us = 180 GB/s (66%). The remaining gap to llama.cpp (159) is the down/o kernels at 66% and the lm_head at 85%; closing it needs a split-k or wider-load variant for k=1024/3072 tiny GEMMs, not a tile retune.
